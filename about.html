About Us

Founded in 2023, The Rustic Fork started with a simple idea: bringing people together over great food. Our chefs are passionate about creating dishes that feel like home but taste like a culinary adventure.

We believe in supporting local farmers, which is why our ingredients are always fresh and seasonal. We look forward to welcoming you into our dining room.
